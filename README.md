# 📊 Global Superstore Sales & 15-Day Forecast | Power BI

> An interactive Power BI analytics dashboard designed to analyze sales, profit, product performance, geographical performance, market contribution, and future sales trends.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes Global Superstore sales data using Microsoft Power BI.
The objective was to transform historical sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps understand:

- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Product performance
- Market performance
- Country-wise sales
- Year-over-year growth
- Top and loss-making products
- Delivery performance
- Short-term sales forecasting

The project also includes a **15-Day Sales Forecast** to provide a forward-looking view of expected sales activity.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

A global retail business needs to understand:

- Which countries generate the most sales?
- Which markets contribute the most revenue?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which products are creating losses?
- How are sales changing year over year?
- What categories contribute the most quantity?
- How quickly are orders being delivered?
- What could sales look like over the next 15 days?

This dashboard was developed to answer these questions through interactive visual analytics.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- Total sales reached approximately **$11.36M**.
- Total profit was approximately **$1.39M**.
- Overall profit margin was approximately **12.19%**.
- Average delivery time was approximately **4 days**.
- The United States generated the highest sales among the displayed countries.
- APAC was the largest market contributor among the displayed markets.
- Product profitability varies significantly across different products.
- Several products generate losses despite being part of the overall sales portfolio.
- Historical sales patterns were used to generate a **15-day sales forecast**.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on Profitable Products

Identify products with consistently high profit and prioritize them in sales and marketing strategies.

### 2. Investigate Loss-Making Products

Analyze pricing, discounts, shipping costs, and product demand for products generating negative profit.

### 3. Strengthen High-Performing Markets

Markets with strong sales contribution can be analyzed further for expansion opportunities.

### 4. Improve Forecast-Based Planning

The 15-day forecast can support short-term inventory, sales, and operational planning.

### 5. Monitor Delivery Performance

With an average delivery time of approximately 4 days, delivery trends should be monitored alongside customer and regional performance.
